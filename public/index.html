Introduction of Us

About Pertubuhan Amal Satu Hati

Pertubuhan Amal Satu Hati is an organization dedicated to bringing hope and support to individuals and communities in need.

Through various charitable, educational, health, and humanitarian efforts, we aim to create a more caring, compassionate, and united society where everyone has the opportunity to live with dignity.

Every programme we run begins the same way — by listening to the people on the ground. From food aid for families going through hard times, to school supplies for children, to medical assistance for those who cannot afford treatment, our work is guided by real needs, not assumptions.

We believe kindness grows when it is shared. Every contribution, no matter the size, becomes part of something bigger — a community that looks after one another.

Our volunteers during a community aid distribution.

What We Do

Four areas where your donation goes to work, every single day.

Social Welfare

Food aid, essentials and emergency support for families going through difficult times.

Education

School supplies, learning programmes and support so no child is left behind.

Health

Medical assistance and health awareness for those who cannot afford treatment.

Humanitarian

Relief efforts during floods, disasters and crises — reaching people fast when it matters.
